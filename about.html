Our story

Calculia EdTech Solutions is a Nigerian-registered EdTech company (RC 9590499) built around one idea: you can't fix a learning gap you haven't diagnosed. Before we teach a single lesson, we identify exactly where a student is struggling — and why.

We support secondary students across five subjects — English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology — with diagnostics, tutoring, mock exams and free video lessons built around the Nigerian curriculum and WAEC, JAMB, NECO and BECE requirements.

Calculia was founded by Opeoluwa Jamiu Olasunkanmi, a Lagos-based educator with 7+ years across public and private secondary schools, whose classroom experience — as an Education Officer at Lagos State TESCOM, a Teaching Fellow at Teach For Nigeria, and Vice Principal at Roche International School — shaped Calculia's diagnostic-first approach to learning.

What We Stand For

🩺 Diagnostic-First
We identify the specific learning difficulty behind a student's struggle before designing a teaching plan around it.
📐 Curriculum Mastery
Deep alignment with NERDC and the Nigerian Scheme of Work across all five subjects we teach.
💻 Ed-Tech Innovation
A digital platform bringing interactive, diagnostic-led STEM learning to students regardless of location.
🏆 Measurable Outcomes
Students consistently achieve strong results in WAEC, NECO, BECE and JAMB with documented performance improvements.
